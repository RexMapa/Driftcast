Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Our commitment

We want Driftcast to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or voice control, and people who are hard of hearing or sensitive to motion. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we already do

  • Semantic page structure with headings, landmarks and a “Skip to content” link on the legal pages
  • Full keyboard access to buttons, sliders and links, with a visible focus outline
  • Respect for the “reduce motion” setting in your device or browser: animations and smooth scrolling are switched off
  • Responsive layout that adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, and supports zooming
  • All generated bridge text is shown on screen as well as read aloud, so nothing depends on audio alone
  • Volume sliders for each ambient sound and a sleep timer with a gentle fade-out
  • Narration uses your device’s own text-to-speech voice, so your speech rate and voice settings apply where your browser supports them
  • Dark, low-glare design that is easier on the eyes at night

Known limitations

We are a small prototype and we know we have more to do. Current issues include:

  • Some small supporting text in the demo (for example hints and time labels) is below the size we would like and may be hard to read
  • Some secondary text uses low-contrast greys on the dark background that may not meet the AA contrast ratio
  • The demo phone screen changes content when you move between steps without announcing the change to screen readers
  • The decorative phone status bar uses emoji icons that are not labelled
  • Text-to-speech availability, voices and pause/resume behaviour vary between browsers and devices, and some do not support it at all
  • We have not yet had the site independently audited

We are working to fix these. This statement was prepared by self-assessment, and we consider the site to be partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 AA.

Tips for a better experience

  • Use your browser’s zoom or text-size settings; the layout is designed to reflow
  • If audio isn’t suitable for you, you can read the generated bridge text and skip playback
  • Keyboard: press Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate them, and the arrow keys to adjust sliders

Feedback and help

If you find a barrier, or need content in another format, please email [your-email@example.com] with the page, what you were trying to do and the device or assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

If you are not happy with our response and you are in the UK, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS). In Northern Ireland, contact the Equality Commission for Northern Ireland.

Technical information

Driftcast relies on HTML, CSS, JavaScript and the Web Audio and Web Speech browser features. It is designed to work with current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
