Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

[Your name or company] operates Driftcast and is the controller of your personal data for the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR. Questions? Email [your-email@example.com].

2. What we collect

We keep this deliberately small. Driftcast has no accounts, no sign-up and no advertising or analytics trackers.

  • What you type. The text you enter in the “What’s keeping you up?” box (up to 220 characters), or the preset you tap, is sent to our server when you press “Curate my sleep-cast”. It may also be sent again, along with an episode title, when you ask for narration. This text can reveal sensitive information such as your health, finances or relationships, so please type only what you are comfortable sharing and avoid names or identifying details.
  • Your IP address. Our server reads your IP address to apply rate limiting and prevent abuse.
  • Standard technical logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records request data such as IP address, time, URL, browser type and referrer for security and operations.
  • Email correspondence. If you contact us, we keep your message and address to reply.

We do not collect your name, email address or payment details through the demo, and we do not use cookies or browser storage (see our Cookie Policy).

3. What stays on your device

Ambient sound (rain, brown noise, fireplace), the sleep timer and the text-to-speech narration all run in your browser. Narration uses your device’s built-in speech voices. We don’t receive any audio from you, and the site never asks for your microphone.

4. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the demo (generate the bridge and episode picks): performance of the service you asked for, and our legitimate interests in running it.
  • To keep the Service secure and affordable (rate limiting, abuse prevention, debugging): legitimate interests.
  • To answer your emails: legitimate interests.
  • To comply with the law where required.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use what you type to build advertising profiles. We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Who we share data with

  • Anthropic, PBC (AI provider): receives the text you type so its model can generate the response. We call its API from our server and never expose our API key to your browser. See Anthropic’s own privacy policy and commercial terms for how it handles API data.
  • Vercel Inc. (hosting and serverless functions): processes requests and logs on our behalf.
  • Upstash (optional rate-limit store): if enabled, stores a short-lived counter keyed to your IP address. Otherwise rate limits are held in the server’s memory only.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where personal data leaves the UK or EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. We may also disclose data if legally required.

6. How long we keep it

  • Typed text is used to generate your response and is not stored by us in a database or account. It may persist briefly in provider logs and in any retention Anthropic applies to API traffic.
  • Rate-limit counters expire after the limit window (about 10 minutes).
  • Hosting logs are kept by Vercel for its standard retention period.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we don’t hold accounts, we may need your IP address and approximate time of use to find anything relating to you. To exercise a right, email us. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Children

Driftcast is not aimed at children under 16 and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, prompts are built on the server, inputs are length-limited and all generated output is escaped before it is displayed. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep what you type general.

10. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above.
