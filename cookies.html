Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. The short version

Driftcast does not set any cookies, and it does not use localStorage, sessionStorage or similar tracking technologies. There are no advertising, analytics or social-media trackers on the site, so there is no cookie banner to accept or reject.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in comparable ways. They are commonly used to remember settings, keep you signed in, measure traffic and show advertising.

3. What we use instead

  • Nothing is remembered between visits. Your typed worry, mix levels and timer only exist in the page while it is open, and are cleared when you close or refresh it.
  • Server-side rate limiting is based on your IP address, not on anything stored on your device.
  • Hosting logs kept by Vercel are server-side records and are not cookies. See the Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties

The site loads all of its scripts, styles and images from its own domain. It does not embed third-party content that could set its own cookies. Some browsers may show a text-to-speech voice from your operating system; that is part of your device, not a cookie.

5. If this changes

If we later add analytics, sign-in or payments, we may start using cookies or similar technologies. Before that, we will update this page and, where the law requires, ask for your consent through a clear banner, with strictly necessary cookies the only ones set without consent.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

You can view, block or delete cookies for any website in your browser’s settings. Doing so won’t affect Driftcast as it currently works.
